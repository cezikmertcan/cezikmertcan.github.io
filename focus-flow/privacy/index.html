Focus Flow

Privacy policy

Policy version: 1.0 · Last updated: 15 September 2026

Focus Flow is a Pomodoro timer published by Mertcan Çezik. It does not require an account, registration, or login.

Data stored on your device

Focus Flow stores timer snapshots, preferences, your selected focus companion, and completed session history on your device. The app has no cloud sync and runs no ads or behavioral analytics. Device backup is controlled by your operating system.

If you allow notifications, Focus Flow schedules local phase-end reminders on your device. It does not send data to a remote notification service.

When you choose Take snapshot, Focus Flow asks to add the generated image to your photo library. The image stays on your device and is not uploaded by Focus Flow.

Purchases

When Pro purchases are enabled, Focus Flow connects to Apple App Store or Google Play and its purchase service to load offerings, complete or restore purchases, and check Pro access. The store and purchase service process an anonymous app installation identifier and purchase history for entitlement delivery and purchase analytics. Focus Flow does not ask for or store your name, email address, or payment details.

Purchase records remain managed by the store account. Refunds and billing disputes are handled by the store that processed the transaction.

Retention and deletion

Local timer data is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its app data. Store purchase records are managed separately by Apple or Google. Because Focus Flow has no account, there is no profile to delete.
