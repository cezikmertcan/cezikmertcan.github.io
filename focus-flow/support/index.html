Support · Updated 16 September 2026 · Published by Mertcan Çezik.

If Focus Flow is not working as expected, email us. Include the app version, device, and a short description of the issue.

Email support

What to include

  • App version and device model.
  • Operating system version and whether notifications are allowed.
  • Steps to reproduce the issue.
  • For a Pro purchase issue, say whether it concerns a purchase or Restore purchases.

Please do not include payment details, passwords, or store credentials.
