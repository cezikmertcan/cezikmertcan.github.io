Support

Support · Updated 24 November 2026 · Published by Mertcan Çezik.

If Keyframe is not working as expected, email us. Include the app version, your device and a short description of the issue.

Email support

What to include

  • App version and device model.
  • iOS version and whether Keyframe can see all photos or only selected ones.
  • Steps to reproduce the issue.
  • For a Pro purchase issue, say whether it concerns a purchase or Restore purchases.

Please do not include payment details, passwords, or store credentials.

Common questions

Where do deleted photos go?

To Recently Deleted in the Photos app. You can restore them there for about 30 days. After that your iPhone removes them for good.

Keyframe does not show all my photos.

You may have allowed only selected photos. Go to Settings → Apps → Keyframe → Photos and choose Full Access, or add more photos there.

How do I get Pro on a new phone?

Sign in with the same Apple account, open Settings → Keyframe Pro in the app and tap Restore purchases.

Why can I only delete 20 photos a day?

That is the free plan’s daily limit. It resets every day. Pro removes the limit, and photos you marked stay saved until you can delete them.
