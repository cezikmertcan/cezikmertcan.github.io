Terms version: 1.0 · Last updated: 24 November 2026

Keyframe is published by Mertcan Çezik and provided for personal use to review and clean up your own photo library. The app is provided as-is.

You decide what to delete. Keyframe deletes items only after you confirm, and they first move to your phone’s Recently Deleted album or trash, where they can be recovered for a limited time, usually 30 days. After that they are removed by your phone. Please keep a backup of photos you care about. Keyframe is not responsible for items you choose to delete.

The free plan lets you review as many photos as you like and delete up to 20 a day. Keyframe Pro removes the daily limit and adds similar-shot, blurry-photo, video and largest-photo cleanup, and suggests the best shot in each group. These checks are estimates; always look before you delete.

Pro is sold through the Apple App Store as a yearly or monthly subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, and you can manage them in your App Store account settings. If a free trial is offered, it turns into a paid subscription when it ends unless you cancel before then. A lifetime purchase does not renew. New users may see a limited-time welcome offer.

The App Store shows the price and applicable taxes before you buy. Refunds, billing disputes and payment details are handled by Apple. Use Restore purchases to get Pro on another device with the same Apple account.

For help, please contact Keyframe support.
