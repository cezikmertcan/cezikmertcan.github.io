Keyframe

Privacy policy

Policy version: 1.0 · Last updated: 24 November 2026

Keyframe is a photo cleanup app published by Mertcan Çezik. It does not require an account, registration or login, and it has no servers that receive your photos.

Your photos and videos

Keyframe reads only the photos and videos you allow in your phone’s photo permission. You can allow your whole library or selected items and change this at any time in your phone’s settings.

To show your library, group similar shots, find blurry and dark photos and large videos, Keyframe looks at your photos, their dates, albums and file sizes on your device. Photos, videos and the results of these checks are never uploaded or shared by Keyframe.

Keyframe deletes items only after you confirm. On iPhone they move to Recently Deleted in the Photos app, and your phone asks for its own confirmation. On Android they move to the system trash where your Android version supports it.

Data stored on your device

Keyframe stores your review progress, keep and delete decisions, review history, photos waiting to be deleted, your daily deletion count and settings on your device. There is no cloud sync, no advertising, no behavioral analytics and no tracking.

On iPhone, Keyframe saves the start time of its welcome offer in the Keychain so the offer does not restart if the app is reinstalled. This entry holds only a date and time and may remain on your device after you delete the app.

If you turn on the weekly reminder, Keyframe schedules a notification on your phone. It is not sent from a server.

Purchases

To show plans, complete or restore purchases and check Pro access, Keyframe connects to the Apple App Store and RevenueCat, its purchase service. They process an anonymous app user identifier and your purchase history to deliver Pro and to analyze purchases. Keyframe does not ask for or store your name, email address or payment details.

Purchase records are managed by your App Store account. Refunds and billing questions are handled by Apple.

Retention and deletion

Data Keyframe keeps in its own storage is removed when you delete the app. Your photos stay in your library unless you choose to delete them. Purchase records are kept by Apple and RevenueCat. Because Keyframe has no account, there is no profile to delete.

Contact

For questions about this policy, please contact Keyframe support.
