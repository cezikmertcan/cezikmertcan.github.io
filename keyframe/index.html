How it works

Small steps. A much lighter library.

Thousands of photos feel like too much. One week of them does not.

01

Week by week

Your gallery is split into weeks. Start a review for one week and see exactly how many photos are in it before you begin.

02

Pick your best shot

Shots taken moments apart are grouped together. Tap through them, keep the best and mark the rest.

03

Review before you delete

Nothing is deleted while you swipe. At the end you see everything you marked and decide once.

Private by design

Your photos never leave your phone.

Keyframe has no servers for your photos, no account and no ads. Everything it does with your library happens on your device.

✳

On-device only

Similar shots, file sizes and your decisions are worked out on your phone. Nothing is uploaded.

✳

No account

There is nothing to sign up for and no profile about you. Open the app and start cleaning.

✳

Undo with Recently Deleted

Deleted photos go to your phone’s Recently Deleted album, so you can still get them back for a while.
